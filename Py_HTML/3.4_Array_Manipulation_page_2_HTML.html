MODULE 2: Advanced Array Manipulation - Stacking and Integration

Mission Briefing: Stacking and Splitting Medical Arrays

🏗️ Building and Breaking: The LEGO Blocks of Data Science

Array stacking is like being a master builder with LEGO blocks! 🧱 Sometimes you need to combine smaller pieces into a larger structure (stacking), and sometimes you need to break apart a big structure into manageable pieces (splitting). In medical research, this happens ALL the time!

🌍 Multi-Site Studies: When Hospitals Unite!

Imagine coordinating a nationwide diabetes study. Each participating hospital sends their patient glucose data separately. Your job? Stack them together for comprehensive analysis! It's like assembling pieces of a puzzle to see the big picture.

Multi-Hospital Glucose Monitoring Study

📚 Vertical Stacking: Building Patient Cohorts

# 📦 Stack vertically to combine all hospitals all_patients = np.vstack([hospital_a_glucose, hospital_b_glucose, hospital_c_glucose]) print("\n🏗️ Combined patient data (3 hospitals × 5 patients each):") print(all_patients) print(f"Total cohort size: {all_patients.size} patients") # 📊 Calculate comprehensive statistics overall_mean = np.mean(all_patients) overall_std = np.std(all_patients) hospital_means = np.mean(all_patients, axis=1) print(f"\n📈 Population Statistics:") print(f" Overall mean glucose: {overall_mean:.1f} mg/dL") print(f" Standard deviation: {overall_std:.1f} mg/dL") print(f" Normal range: 70-100 mg/dL (fasting)") # 🏥 Compare hospitals hospitals = ['A (Urban)', 'B (Suburban)', 'C (Rural)'] for hosp, mean in zip(hospitals, hospital_means): status = "⚠️ Above normal" if mean > 100 else "✅ Normal" print(f" Hospital {hosp}: {mean:.1f} mg/dL {status}")

🔄 Horizontal Stacking: Side-by-Side Comparison

# 📊 Stack horizontally to compare hospitals side by side # First, reshape each array to column format hospital_comparison = np.hstack([ hospital_a_glucose.reshape(-1, 1), hospital_b_glucose.reshape(-1, 1), hospital_c_glucose.reshape(-1, 1) ]) print("\n🔍 Side-by-side comparison (5 patients × 3 hospitals):") print("Patient | Hosp A | Hosp B | Hosp C") print("--------|--------|--------|-------") for i, row in enumerate(hospital_comparison): print(f" {i+1} | {row[0]:3.0f} | {row[1]:3.0f} | {row[2]:3.0f}") # 🎯 Identify patients needing intervention high_glucose_patients = np.where(hospital_comparison > 110) print(f"\n⚠️ Patients with glucose > 110 mg/dL: {len(high_glucose_patients[0])}")
Copied!

Tips:

  • vstack() combines arrays vertically (markers as rows)

  • The | operator means "OR" for boolean arrays
  • np.where() finds indices where condition is True
