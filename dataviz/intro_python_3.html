Why might we use the & operator instead of the word and when combining multiple conditions in Pandas?
The & operator works element-wise on arrays
Correct! The & operator applies the logical AND operation element-wise on arrays of boolean values, which is what we need when filtering DataFrames. The word 'and' would attempt to evaluate the entire condition arrays as single True/False values, which isn't what we want.
The word 'and' is reserved in Python
Not correct. While 'and' is a reserved keyword in Python, that's not why we use & for DataFrame filtering. The reason is that & performs element-wise operations on arrays.
It's just more convenient to type
That's not the reason. In fact, & requires parentheses around each condition which makes it slightly more verbose. We use it because it operates element-wise on arrays.
The & operator is faster
While performance might be better with &, that's not the primary reason. The main reason is that 'and' operates on singular boolean values, while & can operate element-wise on arrays of boolean values.
