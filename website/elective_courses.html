Elective Courses

Personalized Learning Pathways: Electives modules

Our diverse array of elective modules allows students to tailor their precision medicine education to their unique career aspirations. These specialized modules enable you to develop expertise in specific areas that align with your professional goals.

Click on these individual courses to find out more about each elective and how they can enhance your precision medicine journey.

Clinical Perspective in Precision Medicine

Explore the integration of precision medicine approaches in clinical practice and patient care.

Precision Oncology

Learn advanced approaches to cancer treatment through molecular profiling and targeted therapies.

Precision Biomarkers

Discover methods for identifying and validating biomarkers for disease diagnosis and treatment.

Precision Diagnostics

Master cutting-edge diagnostic technologies and their applications in personalized healthcare.

Precision Therapeutics

Understand the development and application of targeted therapeutic strategies.

Precision Drug Discovery and Development

Learn the process of developing targeted drugs from discovery to clinical application.

Causal Inference in Precision Medicine

Apply statistical methods to establish causal relationships in medical research.

Technological Innovation in Precision Medicine

Explore emerging technologies reshaping the future of personalized healthcare.

Precision Health and Medicine for Cardiometabolic diseases

Focus on precision approaches to cardiovascular and metabolic disorders.

Human population health

Study population-level health patterns and their implications for precision medicine.
