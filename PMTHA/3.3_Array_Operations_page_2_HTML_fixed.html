Mission Briefing: Statistical Operations for Evidence-Based Medicine

The Foundation of Medical Research

Welcome to the statistical powerhouse of NumPy! 🚀 These operations are the backbone of every clinical trial, research paper, and treatment guideline. Without statistics, medicine would still be based on guesswork! 🎲❌

Mind-blowing fact: The average drug takes 10-15 years and analyzes data from 3,000+ patients before FDA approval. That's a LOT of statistical calculations! 📈

Why Statistics Matter in Medicine

Statistical operations help us:

  • 📏 Establish normal reference ranges
  • 🎯 Determine treatment effectiveness
  • 🔍 Identify outliers and anomalies
  • 📊 Calculate risk scores
  • 🧪 Validate diagnostic tests

📊 Calculating Key Statistics

# Calculate the statistical story of our patient cohort mean_chol = np.mean(cholesterol_levels) std_chol = np.std(cholesterol_levels) median_chol = np.median(cholesterol_levels) # Percentiles tell us about distribution percentile_25 = np.percentile(cholesterol_levels, 25) percentile_75 = np.percentile(cholesterol_levels, 75) print(f"\n📊 Statistical Summary:") print(f"Mean: {mean_chol:.1f} mg/dL") print(f"Median: {median_chol:.1f} mg/dL") print(f"Std Dev: {std_chol:.1f} mg/dL") print(f"IQR: {percentile_25:.1f} - {percentile_75:.1f} mg/dL")
Copied!
Learning Checkpoint
Which statistical measure is most resistant to outliers?
Mean (np.mean)
Not quite. The mean can be heavily influenced by outliers.
Standard deviation (np.std)
That's not right. Standard deviation is also affected by outliers.
Median (np.median)
Correct! The median is the middle value when data is sorted and is not affected by extreme values (outliers).
Range (max - min)
Not correct. The range is directly determined by outliers (the extreme values).

🚨 Risk Stratification

# Identify patients needing intervention (>200 mg/dL is elevated) high_risk = cholesterol_levels > 200 borderline = (cholesterol_levels >= 180) & (cholesterol_levels <= 200) optimal = cholesterol_levels < 180 # Create a risk report risk_percentage = (np.sum(high_risk) / len(cholesterol_levels)) * 100 print(f"\n🚦 Risk Stratification:") print(f"🟢 Optimal (<180): {np.sum(optimal)} patients") print(f"🟡 Borderline (180-200): {np.sum(borderline)} patients") print(f"🔴 High risk (>200): {np.sum(high_risk)} patients ({risk_percentage:.0f}%)") # Statistical significance check if risk_percentage > 50: print("\n⚠️ Alert: Majority of cohort at elevated risk!") print("📋 Recommendation: Implement clinic-wide lifestyle intervention program")

Lab Exercise 3: Analyzing Treatment Response in Clinical Trials

Your Mission

You're the lead data scientist for a clinical trial! 📊 Analyze blood pressure changes after 4 weeks of a new antihypertensive drug. Your analysis will determine if this treatment gets FDA approval! 💊✅

Stakes are high: Your analysis could help millions with hypertension! 🌍

Clinical Trial Design

  • Baseline: BP before treatment
  • Post-treatment: BP after 4 weeks
  • Success criteria: >10% reduction = responder
  • FDA requirement: >60% response rate for approval

Your Challenge

# Lab scenario: Paired clinical trial data 🧪 # Each patient serves as their own control (powerful design!) baseline_bp = np.array([145, 152, 138, 160, 142, 155, 148, 162, 140, 158]) post_treatment_bp = np.array([128, 135, 130, 142, 125, 138, 132, 145, 124, 140]) # 🎯 TODO: Your analysis tasks # 1. Calculate absolute BP reduction for each patient # 2. Calculate percentage reduction # 3. Determine treatment response rate (>10% reduction = responder) # 4. Create statistical summary of treatment effect # 5. Make FDA recommendation based on results # 💡 Hint: Think about both individual and population-level effects!

Learning Checkpoint
To calculate the absolute BP reduction for each patient, which NumPy operation should you use?
baseline_bp + post_treatment_bp
Not quite. Addition would give you the sum, not the reduction.
baseline_bp - post_treatment_bp
Correct! Absolute reduction is calculated by subtracting post-treatment values from baseline values.
baseline_bp * post_treatment_bp
That's not right. Multiplication won't give you the reduction.
baseline_bp / post_treatment_bp
Not correct. Division gives you a ratio, not the absolute reduction.

Complete Clinical Trial Analysis

# Complete clinical trial analysis with FDA-style reporting! 📊 baseline_bp = np.array([145, 152, 138, 160, 142, 155, 148, 162, 140, 158]) post_treatment_bp = np.array([128, 135, 130, 142, 125, 138, 132, 145, 124, 140]) print("🧪 CLINICAL TRIAL ANALYSIS REPORT") print("=" * 50) print(f"Trial Size: {len(baseline_bp)} patients") print(f"Treatment Duration: 4 weeks") # Calculate treatment effects absolute_reduction = baseline_bp - post_treatment_bp percent_reduction = (absolute_reduction / baseline_bp) * 100 # Identify responders (>10% reduction is clinically meaningful) responders = percent_reduction > 10 response_rate = (np.sum(responders) / len(baseline_bp)) * 100 # Statistical summary mean_reduction = np.mean(absolute_reduction) std_reduction = np.std(absolute_reduction) median_reduction = np.median(absolute_reduction) # Effect size calculation (Cohen's d) effect_size = mean_reduction / std_reduction print(f"\n📊 PRIMARY OUTCOMES:") print(f"Mean BP reduction: {mean_reduction:.1f} ± {std_reduction:.1f} mmHg") print(f"Median reduction: {median_reduction:.1f} mmHg") print(f"Range: {np.min(absolute_reduction):.0f} - {np.max(absolute_reduction):.0f} mmHg") print(f"Effect size (Cohen's d): {effect_size:.2f} {'(Large)' if effect_size > 0.8 else '(Medium)'}") print(f"\n📈 RESPONSE ANALYSIS:") print(f"Treatment responders: {np.sum(responders)}/{len(baseline_bp)} ({response_rate:.0f}%)") print(f"FDA threshold: 60% (Requirement {'MET ✅' if response_rate >= 60 else 'NOT MET ❌'})") # Individual patient outcomes print(f"\n👥 PATIENT-LEVEL RESULTS:") for i in range(len(baseline_bp)): emoji = "✅" if responders[i] else "➖" print(f"Patient {i+1}: {baseline_bp[i]} → {post_treatment_bp[i]} " f"(-{absolute_reduction[i]} mmHg, -{percent_reduction[i]:.1f}%) {emoji}") # Clinical significance analysis clinically_significant = absolute_reduction >= 10 print(f"\n🏥 CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:") print(f"Clinically significant responses (≥10 mmHg): {np.sum(clinically_significant)}/{len(baseline_bp)}") # Safety analysis excessive_reduction = percent_reduction > 20 print(f"\n⚠️ SAFETY MONITORING:") print(f"Patients with >20% reduction: {np.sum(excessive_reduction)}") if np.sum(excessive_reduction) > 0: print("Note: Monitor these patients for hypotension symptoms") # FDA recommendation print(f"\n📋 RECOMMENDATION:") if response_rate >= 60 and mean_reduction >= 10: print("✅ APPROVE: Treatment demonstrates both statistical and clinical significance") print(f" - Response rate: {response_rate:.0f}% (exceeds 60% threshold)") print(f" - Mean reduction: {mean_reduction:.1f} mmHg (clinically meaningful)") elif response_rate >= 50: print("🟡 CONDITIONAL: Consider approval with post-market surveillance") else: print("❌ REJECT: Insufficient evidence of efficacy") # Number Needed to Treat (NNT) nnt = 1 / (response_rate / 100) print(f"\n💊 Number Needed to Treat (NNT): {nnt:.1f}") print(f"(We need to treat ~{int(np.ceil(nnt))} patients for 1 to benefit)")
