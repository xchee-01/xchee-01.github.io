🔬 Displaying Vital Signs Like a Pro

In medical settings, displaying multiple pieces of information clearly can be life-saving. Let's explore different ways to present vital signs:

Method 1: Sequential Display

# Patient vital signs - clear and simple patient_name = "Emma Thompson" heart_rate = 72 temperature = 98.6 print("Patient:", patient_name) print("Heart Rate:", heart_rate, "bpm") print("Temperature:", temperature, "°F")
Copied!

💡 Medical Insight: A normal resting heart rate for adults ranges from 60 to 100 bpm. Athletes often have rates as low as 40 bpm!

Method 2: Formatted Strings (The Professional Touch)

F-strings are like having a smart formatting assistant - they make your output look polished and professional:

# Professional vital signs display patient_id = "PT-2024-789" oxygen_saturation = 98 blood_pressure = "120/80" print(f"╔══════════════════════════════════╗") print(f"║ Patient ID: {patient_id} ║") print(f"║ O2 Saturation: {oxygen_saturation}% ║") print(f"║ Blood Pressure: {blood_pressure} mmHg ║") print(f"╚══════════════════════════════════╝")
Copied!

🔬 Advanced Output Techniques

Let's explore some advanced tricks to make your medical programs even more impressive!

Creating Progress Indicators

When processing medical data, showing progress keeps users informed:

# Analyzing patient data with progress indicator print("Analyzing blood sample", end="") print(".", end="") print(".", end="") print(". Complete! ✅")
Copied!

This outputs: Analyzing blood sample... Complete! ✅

Multi-line Medical Reports

For complex reports, you can use triple quotes:

# Comprehensive test results patient = "John Doe" test_date = "2024-01-15" report = f""" ╔════════════════════════════════════════╗ ║ LABORATORY TEST REPORT ║ ╠════════════════════════════════════════╣ ║ Patient: {patient} ║ ║ Date: {test_date} ║ ║ ║ ║ BLOOD PANEL RESULTS: ║ ║ • Glucose: 92 mg/dL (Normal) ║ ║ • Cholesterol: 180 mg/dL (Normal) ║ ║ • Hemoglobin: 14.5 g/dL (Normal) ║ ╚════════════════════════════════════════╝ """ print(report)
Copied!
