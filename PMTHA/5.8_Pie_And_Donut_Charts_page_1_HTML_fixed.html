Expected output:

A professional pie chart displaying: - Six colored slices representing disease categories - Each slice labeled with the disease name - Percentages shown on each slice (e.g., 31.3% for Cardiovascular) - Title: "Annual Disease Diagnosis Distribution" with "Total Patients: 4,000" - Equal aspect ratio (circular, not oval) - Professional color scheme appropriate for medical presentations

Answer:
