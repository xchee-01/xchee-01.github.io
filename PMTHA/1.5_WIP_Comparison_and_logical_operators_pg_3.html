Mission Briefing: The Logic of Medical Decisions

Medical decision-making rarely depends on just one factor. It's like being a detective solving a complex case - you need multiple clues that work together! That's where logical operators come in.

Your Master-Level Clinical Tools:

  • and: "ALL conditions must be true" (strict like ICU protocols)
  • or: "AT LEAST ONE condition must be true" (flexible like emergency criteria)
  • not: "The OPPOSITE of what you said" (exclusion criteria)

These operators mirror exactly how doctors think:

  • AND thinking: "Patient needs surgery IF they have the condition AND are stable AND have no contraindications"
  • OR thinking: "Patient needs immediate care IF they have chest pain OR difficulty breathing OR are unconscious"
  • NOT thinking: "Patient is eligible IF they do NOT have allergies AND do NOT have contraindications"

Let's start with the basics:

# Basic AND logic - like checking if you can go outside is_sunny = True have_sunglasses = True can_enjoy_sunshine = is_sunny and have_sunglasses print("Ready for sunshine:", can_enjoy_sunshine)

The NOT Operator: Exclusion Criteria and Safety Checks

The not operator is like that colleague who always says the opposite of what you expect. In medicine, it's crucial for checking the absence of conditions or creating exclusion criteria.

Medication Contraindication Check

# Basic NOT operation for medication safety has_allergies = False safe_for_medication = not has_allergies # Safe if NO allergies print("Safe to give medication:", safe_for_medication)
